Privacy Policy

Nudge · last updated June 11, 2026

1. Summary

Nudge (“the app”) is a free reminder and notification app that helps you stay on top of your tasks. It is published by Cool App Helpdesk.

  • The developer collects no personal data. There are no user accounts and no developer-operated analytics or tracking systems.
  • The app includes an optional in-app feedback feature that sends your message to a developer-operated endpoint — see section 5.
  • Your reminders, categories, and settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere unless you explicitly submit feedback.

2. Data we collect

None. Nudge does not automatically collect personal data, telemetry, identifiers, device information, IP addresses, or usage analytics. There are no user accounts and the developer operates no analytics or ad-serving infrastructure that the app communicates with automatically.

If you choose to submit feedback via the in-app feedback form, the message, subject, feedback type, and optional contact information you provide are sent to a developer-operated endpoint. This data is used solely to respond to your feedback and improve the app. See section 5 for details.

Nudge requires certain permissions to deliver timely reminders and notifications:

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, phone state, or any other sensitive permission beyond those listed above.

5. Network activity and third parties

Nudge communicates with one developer-operated endpoint solely for the optional in-app feedback feature:

  • Endpoint: https://requests-847893873240.europe-west1.run.app (Google Cloud Run)
  • Purpose: Receive feedback messages (feature requests, bug reports, general inquiries)
  • Data sent: Feedback type, subject, message body, and optional contact information — only when you explicitly tap Submit

There are no third-party SDKs in the app. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social, or attribution SDKs are included. The app does not serve ads, collect telemetry, or transmit any data automatically.

6. Children’s privacy

Nudge is not specifically directed at children, but the developer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR’s provisions for children (GDPR-K). There is no profile, no account, no chat, and no in-app purchases.

Parents who have questions about their child’s privacy can contact us at coolapphelpdesk@gmail.com.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the developer does not automatically collect any data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Feedback messages you submit are stored in the developer-operated endpoint and are retained only as long as needed to respond and address the feedback. To request deletion of your feedback data, contact us at coolapphelpdesk@gmail.com.

To remove locally stored data (reminders, categories, history, settings), clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Nudge → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

The app stores reminder data and preferences locally in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. Network requests for feedback submission are made over HTTPS. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
